* Basic unit of all kinds of language models are referred to as "Tokens".
* **Self Supervised learning of Modern Language models**: Instead of relying on humans to label every training example, the model creates its own targets from raw, unlabeled text.
* Most modern models like GPT and Claude are autoregressive: They generate text sequentially. While, older models like BERT are masked and look at text bidirectionally to fill in the blanks.
* **Parameter**: "Parameter" a variable in a model which is constantly updated through training.
* **Foundation Models**: Called so due to their breakthroughs in research.
* **Multimodal Models**: Multimodal models are AI systems designed to process, understand, and generate information across multiple types of data—such as text, images, audio, and video—within a single unified architecture.
* **Fine Tuning**: use fine tuning when we want that model to work on something that was not included in it's training.
* Quantization levels of Models:
	* FP32 - 32 Bits - 1x smaller
	* FP16 - 16 Bits - 2x
	* BF16 - 16 bits - 2x
	* FP8 - 8 Bits - 4x
	* INT8 - 8 Bits - 4x
	* INT4 - 4 Bits - 8x
	* INT2 - 1-2 Bits - 16x-32x
